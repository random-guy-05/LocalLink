LOCAL LINK / NATIVE ENGINE PREVIEW / 0.4.1

Your other Mac.
Right here.

LocalLink now includes its own screen capture, hardware video engine, sound and remote control. No Sunshine or Moonlight is needed. Keep Tailscale connected on both Macs.

1. Install on both Macs

Drag LocalLink into Applications on each Mac. If macOS blocks this ad-hoc signed preview, follow Apple’s Open Anyway instructions. This preview is not notarized.

2. On your Mac mini

  1. Open LocalLink and choose Share this Mac.
  2. Enable LocalLink under Privacy & Security → Screen & System Audio Recording and under Accessibility. Permissions given to Sunshine do not carry over.
  3. Quit and reopen LocalLink after granting Screen Recording.
  4. Click Start sharing this Mac, then Copy key. Keep the mini awake, signed in, and LocalLink open.

3. On your viewing Mac

  1. Enter the mini’s Tailscale IP or hostname.
  2. Paste the pairing key from the mini and click Connect to Mac.
  3. Click the remote picture to control it. The toolbar has full screen and disconnect controls. ⌘ ⇧ D disconnects.

Anyone with the key can connect while sharing is on. Keep it private. A new key revokes the previous one. Pairing secrets are stored in macOS Keychain. LocalLink listens on TCP port 48100; no router port forwarding is needed with Tailscale.

Preview status

Encrypted transport and synthetic hardware video tests pass. A real two-Mac stream still needs validation. This is an early native engine: single display, SDR, no clipboard or file transfer, and no guarantee of delivered FPS or near-native latency. If HEVC fails, switch it off under Picture & sound to use H.264.
